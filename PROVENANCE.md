# Provenance and methods

Original circuit: Fleming-Society/PCB-Challenge-2026. The supplied PCB/schematic/project source bytes are retained under source. Captured official instructions at revision 0a569d9d622c51f90f7036a9ef8f5efa4f112eae are included under requirements. Source and bundled KiCad library attribution are retained; no new licence is asserted over them.

Routing proposals and local analysis were prepared with Codex and scripted CAD tools. Eligibility under the official no-auto-trace condition has not been established. All original parts, literal net identities and embedded footprint geometry remain present. Some original custom footprint libraries were not supplied; see project/MISSING-LIBRARIES.md.

The current candidate is incomplete. Native DRC is included, while complete supply/reference/USB/GND, logic, stack/electrical and rule checks are unfinished. The separately protected accepted fallback and full local engineering records are not replaced by this repository.
